### Results / table / calendar
- Archive shows **"5:0 vs LÜB (A)"** for a 0:5 *loss*: `formatFixtureResult` (`src/sim/league-week.ts:98`)
  always writes home goals first.
- Calendar at week 34 showed the next fixture on **Sat 29 Aug "Heim vs TÖN"**; TÖN was actually MD1 and
  ITZ was played on 29 Aug. Label is hard-coded German with short codes (`month-grid.ts:77`).
- **MD1 was simulated without lineup or broadcast.** Root cause: `simulateGameWeek` played the matchday of
  the *next* week (`advanceLeagueWeek(…, nextWeek)`), so MD1 ran blind during preseason week 0 (where
  `prepareCareerMatch` refuses), and every later matchday sat one Saturday earlier than the calendar showed.
  Fixed 2026-10-21 (pipeline plays the ending week's matchday).
- Short codes (TÖN, ITZ, LÜB, KIE …) everywhere in archive / calendar / news. **Rule (user): never show slugs
  or short forms, always the full club name.**
- Stadium is called **"Nordstern Arena"** while the club is Kieler FC.

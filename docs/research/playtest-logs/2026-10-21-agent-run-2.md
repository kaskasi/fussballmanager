## Friction log

- The good stuff is hidden: CA 109 U19 and CA 115/116 camp strikers exist from day one, but the only
  promotion path is profile → contract → "Profikader" (U17 even needs "In U19" first). In run 1 the
  inbox offered Luis Klein in week 41 — far too late and easy to defer.
- Camp roster button "HQ" is not explained; the U17 count jumped 18 → 36 after the first pull.
- Camps occupy both construction slots → no training-ground / stadium upgrades in the first two weeks,
  which the route also needs. Buying two camps breaks the board cash objective immediately (the modal does
  warn: "Vorstand-Kasse verfehlt").
- Scouting: "Scout region" is disabled while any recon runs, even with 3 scouts — "daily scouting" means
  one click per day on a screen far from the desk.
- Outgoing transfers list the same player twice (BID + NEGOTIATION cards).
- Training plan still empty every Monday (see run 1).
- All modals of the route are German in EN (camp buy, contract, profile).

## Balance note

The survival route works but is narrow and front-loaded with knowledge the game does not surface. Options
for PRD 11.11 / 22:
1. Surface "ready" youth and camp headliners in the week-33 inbox (promote/keep decision on day one).
2. Let the first camp not consume a construction slot, or give a starter third slot.
3. Soften season-1 rival CA (or the board target) so autopilot ends ~14th and the route is a reward, not a requirement.
